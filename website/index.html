Free. Works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari with a keyboard and mouse.

What's in the realm

The full Oryx gauntlet

When a realm closes, follow Oryx from his Castle through his Chamber, Wine Cellar, Sanctuary and Hash Cellar to the Doomed Cellar. Every stage is harder, and the loot gets better all the way down.

Doomed Realms content

Items, pets, classes, dungeons and minigames from Doomed Realms, alongside the classic game's gear, with item tiers that glow.

Dungeons that work

Dozens of dungeons made playable, random layouts, rebuilt Elder dungeons, and a Nexus Bazaar to spend your fame in.

Updates without kicks

Server updates never disconnect you. You keep playing, dungeon runs included, and switch to the new version at your next trip to the Nexus.

Browser or desktop

The same account and characters in both. Start in the browser now, and download the client later if you want it.

Guilds, parties and trading

Party up, run a guild, trade or sell on the marketplace, and keep your best gear in your vault and pets at your side.

Get started

  1. Open the game. Press Play in your browser, or download the client.
  2. Register. The game opens on its sign-in screen: pick Register, choose a name and a password.
  3. Choose a class and you're in the Nexus. Take a realm portal and start leveling.

Download

The desktop client keeps itself up to date: new versions download in the background and switch over at your next portal. You only download it once.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There's nothing to buy.

Can I play on my phone?

Not really: the game needs a keyboard and mouse. Any computer with a recent browser works.

The browser version won't start.

It needs WebGL 2. Update your browser and turn on hardware acceleration in its settings, or download the client instead.

Are browser and desktop accounts the same?

Yes. Sign in with the same name and password in either, and your characters, vault and fame are all there.

Windows warns about the download.

The client isn't signed with a paid certificate, so Windows SmartScreen may say it doesn't recognise it. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
