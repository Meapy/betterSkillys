Endgame

Six stages down

When a realm closes, Oryx runs. Follow him through six stages, each harder than the last, to the Doomed Cellar at the bottom. The best loot is waiting down there.

  1. I

    Oryx's Castle

    Opens when a realm closes. Get past the Stone Guardians to reach him.

  2. II

    Oryx's Chamber

    Oryx 1 and his dance. His fall leaves a locked portal behind.

  3. III

    Wine Cellar

    Oryx 2. A Wine Cellar Incantation opens the way. He calls his guards at half health.

  4. IV

    Oryx's Sanctuary

    Oryx 3 and his court. Get far away when he shouts "KNEEL!".

  5. V

    Hash Cellar

    Oryx 4. Your first real chance at the Oryx set.

  6. VI

    Doomed Cellar

    Oryx 5. He can't be hurt until his Trees of Doom fall. Every kill is announced to the server.

Help

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There's nothing to buy.

Can I play on my phone?

Not really: the game needs a keyboard and mouse. Any computer with a recent browser works.

The browser version won't start.

It needs WebGL 2. Update your browser and turn on hardware acceleration in its settings, or download the desktop client.

Are browser and desktop accounts the same?

Yes. Sign in with the same name and password in either, and your characters, vault and fame are all there.

Windows warns about the download.

The client isn't signed with a paid certificate, so SmartScreen may say it doesn't recognise it. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
